Guess Who’s Back!

Napstr is bringing music sharing back with modern privacy. Searchable catalogues are published through Nostr while audio transfers travel through authenticated, temporary Tor onion services. There is no central Napstr tracker and no central file store. Read about the Napstr privacy model.

How Napstr Works

Pick a folder to share and Napstr recursively indexes supported audio files with SHA-256. Other people find the public catalogue, request a file privately, and verify every chunk they receive. Your files stay on your computer until you choose to share them. Discovery uses Nostr; downloads travel through temporary Tor onion services, so file-transfer peers do not receive your direct IP address. Only copyleft music should be shared.
